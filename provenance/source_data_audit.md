# Source-data provenance audit

This audit records publication-relevant material from archived `gcmmagicc/data` snapshots and the authoritative selections made from the current manuscript. Paths are normalized repository-relative paths; large archives, checkpoints, databases, caches, and raw model output are not copied into this repository.

## Promoted material

- **Emergent constraints:** `plots.tar` and `plotsT1.tar` are byte-identical 559,450,108-byte archives with SHA-256 `43923f2cf9563ec19273ad98a7422737448749ed7f8d6bddb92e5e590f8ef36c`. The release contains the 799-row trend table covering 19 models, the six ERA5-conditioned tables with 30/39/48/15/12/57 rows, Nicolai Meinshausen's original sampler, the completed seven-quantile table, a normalized composite, and the canonical PDF/PNG. Prepared-data-to-figure reproduction is complete. Generation of the prepared trends from raw CMIP6, ERA5, and GCMagicc-PM checkpoints is unavailable and unverified.
- **Resolution sensitivity:** although the archived output lives below a legacy `plotsT1` path, the bundled generator, configuration, and checkpoint references identify the production A5 lineage, whose public name is GCMagicc. Normalized metadata and ten time-index-1022 synthesis PDFs are frozen. Large per-map JSON and checkpoints remain external under `gcmagicc-checkpoints`.
- **Aerosol sensitivity:** although the archived output lives below a legacy `plotsT1` path, the bundled generator, configuration, and checkpoint references identify the production A5 lineage, whose public name is GCMagicc. The diagnostic is the full-forcing minus zero-aerosol-ERF change in diurnal temperature range (`tasmax - tasmin`) in K at `nside=64`, using 100 stochastic samples. Eleven compact maps, the combined matrix, and normalized source scripts are frozen. The period is provisionally 2015--2024.
- **GCMagicc-XS:** the ten manuscript PNGs are frozen. The historical 1,528,623,568-byte monthly CSV is not redistributed in v1.0.1, but its exact size and SHA-256 are retained so it can be added in a future Zenodo version if requested. A deterministic 205,407-row plotted-point sample makes compact replotting release-native; full raw recomputation continues to require `gcmagicc-xs-bundle` and the non-redistributed normalized inputs.
- **Validation and observational alignment:** the release freezes the current main validation artifact `validation_diagnostics_main_v100_20260821_1121` and supplementary alternate draws `s01`--`s10` from the same publication set. Its provenance records the exact generator hashes, publication manifest and table sources, the 12.1-GB metrics database (`70cba2cb...`), and the promoted 2.7-GB energy-distance database (`0bae785e...`); both databases remain external, while their immutable rebuild validation record is committed. The three older SSP2-4.5 SCOREEDISTC panels, three observation-referenced EDISTO panels, and observational-alignment artifact remain tied to the historical 11.9-GB metrics audit. The observational-alignment source is the authoritative bundle `observational_alignment_v100_20260827_221422`, generated immediately before commit `c5c3d9f`, which contains the exact generator content. Relative to the August 7 bundle, only the generated timestamp, output paths, and the displayed `n100 CMIP7 ScenarioMIP` label changed; scientific arrays, panel-c model indexes, panel diagnostics, and row-5 match-map inputs are unchanged. The validation figure's modified-worktree provenance and the observational-alignment figure's pre-commit generation context are recorded separately with exact generator and artifact hashes.
- **SPEI sensitivity:** provenance is anchored to revision `16c9d72`, which integrates revisions `8a3bb76` and `34581af` while retaining the established pathway default and plotting semantics. The semantic Iran PDF and normalized compact tables are frozen.

## Recorded without promotion

- **Crop failure:** excluded because the authoritative manuscript no longer uses it.
- **Separate ten-variable scenario projections:** audit-only because no corresponding figure is active in the authoritative manuscript.
- **Older A5 archive:** inventory-only because it is a redundant historical archive; the selected A5/GCMagicc resolution and aerosol artifacts and their exact source hashes are already frozen separately.
- **Duplicate archives, caches, database copies, and operational intermediates:** inventory-only. They are mutable or redundant and are not suitable release artifacts.
